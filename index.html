BOOKING & PAYMENT

Please read before making payment. A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking.

Rescheduling: request a change by 16:00 Bali time the day before your session. Cancellations after this deadline, including last-minute cancellations, count as a used session and are non-refundable.

Refunds are available for: unsuitable weather; an accident (medical bill or doctor's receipt required); last-minute flight changes (new flight booking required); or cancellation by the coach.

COACH ASSIGNMENT

Coaches may vary between sessions due to booking demand and scheduling. Hanging Surf Club reserves the right to rotate coaches when needed.

To request a specific coach or the same coach as your previous session, please tell us at least 24 hours before your session, for group or private lessons. Coach changes or requests cannot be made less than 24 hours before the session.

For group classes, requesting the same coach as your previous session carries an additional IDR 100,000 fee and is treated as a private session. Requests are subject to coach availability.
